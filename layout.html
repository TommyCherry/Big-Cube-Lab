    Prototype scope and limits

    Redux searches U, F, Rw, 3Rw, 4Rw, 5Rw, Lw, 3Lw, 4Lw, and 5Lw. Yau searches U, Rw, 3Rw, 4Rw, and 5Rw. Both include primes and doubles, each costing one move. A result is shortest within that move set, not all cube moves. Yau preserves all FL/DL/BL edge pieces as they are when search starts.

    All six centers must return to their original faces. Edges and corners are not solved. IDA* uses exact small pattern tables to prune paths and avoids retaining the entire search frontier. Search stops at the chosen depth or time limit. There is no state cap; Cancel remains available. Longer scrambles can exceed these limits. No case database is generated.

    Ergonomic search uses setup + U/U'/U2 + inverse setup blocks, with standalone U adjustments before, between, or after blocks. Setups are Rw through 5Rw or individual slices 2r through 5r, with primes allowed. Each slice counts as one move; adjacent wide/slice turns are combined when possible. An optional final commutator solves the last exchanged oblique pair, including verified symmetric/inverse variants and U/F alignment. In Yau, F actions use safe wide-turn equivalents. It minimizes written moves after adjacent turns combine, including across the final commutator boundary, not the number of blocks. This is optimal within those blocks and the supported finishing commutators; its slice-turn move set differs from the ordinary move-optimal search.

    Search rules and optimality

    Each Rw, 3Rw, l or U turn counts as one move, including primes and doubles. Half centers and Full 1st center minimize stage 1 first, select the first shortest route found, then minimize the finish from that endpoint. Tied stage-1 routes are not ranked by their finish length. Half centers accepts all four cyclic color arrangements around the fixed L/R axis and preserves the three bars during its Rw/U finish; the solved orientation is shown with the result. Full 1st center chooses whichever center can be solved quickest in its original face position; its finish can temporarily disturb it. Globally optimal minimizes the whole solution to the original face colors without requiring either milestone first. Cancelling stops the search without an optimality result.

    F3E solver

    Solve the quickest first edge, then the quickest compatible second edge, then the third. Once an edge is solved, every later move must leave it solved in the same slot.

    Three-stage mode minimizes each stage while preserving earlier edges after every move; its total is not guaranteed globally optimal. Globally optimal minimizes the entire solution without those intermediate locks. U, F, D, B, 3Rw, Rw, R and L are allowed; primes and doubles each count as one move. No time or move limit; cancel any time.

    F3E goal

    Pair three white edges at FL, DL and BL. Their side colors must follow the cube's color order.

      Valid relative orders

      Any three of the four white edges may be used, including white-red. White faces L in every row; the remaining white edge is unrestricted. UL is not a target.

      Adds Rw2 D Rw' U2 Rw D' Rw' U2 Rw' and x Rw2 D2 Rw U2 Rw' D2 Rw U2 Lw, plus inverse and y-rotation variants, as complete blocks. Rw2' is equivalent to Rw2.         L8E solution finder

        Optimal block-cost mode searches complete blocks exhaustively by their face/wide-turn cost: R U R' costs 3; U or Uw costs 1; primes and doubles have the same cost. Cube rotations cost 0. Costs are added before cancellations between blocks; the simplified algorithm's move count is shown separately. A result is called optimal only after all cheaper block sequences are ruled out. On timeout, any verified best solution is shown without an optimality claim.

        Heuristic search using AUFs, Uw slices, traditional U-turn inserts. Sledges are used only inside slice-flip-slice algorithms. Favors three-pair E-layer setups for slice / replace / unslice pairing, including slice-first starts. Slice-flip-slice variants handle difficult finishes. Back-face inserts are shown with y/y' rotations into R/L inserts, restoring the grip after each block. Rotations are reported separately from move count. Not guaranteed optimal.

        The D cross is preserved after each complete block; centers may be temporarily sliced. The finish restores all centers and pairs all eight dedges, without requiring their final 3x3 positions. Heuristic search stops after 30 seconds, 2 million visits or 40 blocks. Exact mode has a 30-second time budget, including candidate search, with no beam, visit or block-depth cutoff. Every result is replay-checked, and removable blocks are deleted.
